Looking for serial ports…

The Bus Servo Adapter (A) shows up as a USB serial device with a WCH CH34x chip (Linux: /dev/ttyACM* or /dev/ttyUSB*, macOS: /dev/cu.*). Set its jumper to USB mode and power the servos through its DC input.
